# 6. Any signed-in user can annotate; seed with public-domain art

Date: 2026-10-01 · Status: Accepted

## Context

The project's point of difference is community annotation, as on Genius. A public demo also needs content on day one without copyright problems.

## Decision

- Any signed-in user can annotate any image. Authors can edit or delete their own content.
- Seed the demo with **public-domain artwork** from museum open-access collections.
- Uploaders confirm they have the rights to an image, and every image has a report/takedown link.

## Consequences

- The community model works from the start, with a minimal moderation surface (author delete and reports).
- Artwork gives rich detail to annotate and avoids licensing issues for seed content.
- Voting and reputation (post-MVP) will be needed once annotations on one image compete.
